# Freedom Broker (Kazakhstan) — Support-QA Knowledge Base

Compiled 2026-11-07 from public sources only. Retail clients in Kazakhstan are served by **Freedom Finance Global PLC** (AIFC, Astana; AFSA licence AFSA-A-LA-2020-0019; 100% owned by Freedom Holding Corp.), trading as **Freedom Broker**. Help centre `help.freedombroker.kz` now 301-redirects to `fbroker.kz/faq`; `ffin.kz` redirects to `fbroker.kz`. Primary normative document: *Регламент оказания брокерских (агентских) услуг* (version 30.04.2026) and its *Приложение 3 — Комиссии, тарифы и возмещения* (approved 30.04.2026). Facts the operator states should match these; items marked **UNVERIFIED** could not be confirmed on an official page.

## 1. Tariff plans and commissions

- Official plan names (RU): «Старт», «Инвестор», «Стандарт», «Стандарт Плюс», «Международный», «Инвестиционная подписка», «Подписка трейдера». There is **no plan called «Smart» or «Все включено»** on fbroker.kz. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/tariffs-fees-and-commissions2708) (page updated Nov 2026)
- «Инвестор» is the default public tariff (Annex 3 §D). Any tariff change takes effect **from the next business day** unless the Company's notice says otherwise (Annex 3 §E). [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/2026/05/04/3-prilozenie-3-tarify-ffg-pravki-new-ru-kz-163207.pdf)
- «Инвестор» rates: KASE/AIX (incl. IPO) 0.085%; international markets 0.5% + $0.012/security + $1.2 per (partly) executed order; ITS 0.5% (min $0.01) + $0.012/security; HKEX 0.7% + $0.012 + $1.2; options $10/order + $3/contract; no subscription fee. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/investor-tariff)
- «Старт»: promo for first-ever brokerage account opened from 22 May 2024; 0.05% on KASE/AIX, international and ITS; valid 30 days, then auto-switch to «Инвестор» with no client action; not granted for second/re-opened accounts. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/start-tariff)
- «Стандарт»: international 0.12% + $1.2/order; ITS 0.12% min $0.01; HKEX 0.32% + $1.2; options $10 + $1/contract; "available by agreement with the Company". [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/standard-tariff) Finratings (16 Mar 2026) adds the broker reviews requests only with assets ≥ $500 — **UNVERIFIED** on official page. [source](https://finratings.kz/news/12609-kak-platit-menshe-komissii-za-sdelki-kakie-tarify-est-u-freedom-broker/)
- «Стандарт Плюс»: international 0.25% + $1.2; ITS 0.25% min $0.01; options $10 + $2; connected on a tariff-change order. «Международный»: 0.38% + $0.012 + $1.2; at Company's discretion. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/tariffs-fees-and-commissions2708)
- «Инвестиционная подписка»: $10 per calendar month; international 0.2% + $1.2; ITS 0.2% min $0.01. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/investment-subscription-tariff)
- «Подписка трейдера»: $35 per calendar month; same 0.2% + $1.2 schedule; debited from the brokerage account on the first business day of each month, pro-rated for mid-month sign-ups; cancelling = changing tariff via Menu → Orders → Account Settings → Change Tariff (loses Telegram access). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/trader)
- Change tariff path (app): «Меню → Поручения → Настройки счета → Смена тарифного плана»; takes effect next business day. [source](https://finratings.kz/news/12609-kak-platit-menshe-komissii-za-sdelki-kakie-tarify-est-u-freedom-broker/)
- **How to see your tariff**: app «Меню → Отчеты и документы → Отчеты брокера → Получить отчет»; web (tradernet.global) «Кабинет → Отчеты брокера»; the tariff is shown in the report's «Account information / Информация о счете» block. There is no separate "tariff" screen in a «Кабинет» tab of the app. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/how-to-find-out-your-tariff-2709)
- Inactive-account fee ($50, Reg. cl. 4.4) **abolished 9 Jan 2026**. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/is-there-a-commission-for-an-inactive-account-)
- KCSD custody fee for KASE holdings is set by the Central Depository, debited monthly in the following month. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/custody-fees-of-the-securities)
- Other Annex 3 fees: margin loan carried overnight 15% p.a. (USD-denominated) for foreign-currency cash, 18.5% p.a. for KZT, 15% for borrowed securities, **zero if repaid within the trading day**; OTC: debt instruments 0.3%, KCSD-settled 0.2%, AIX CSD 0.25%, ITS CSD $10 (DTC)/$12 (Euroclear)/$30 (HK), other 2% min $25; OTC IPO participation 5%/4%/3% by ticket size, sale 0.5%. [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/2026/05/04/3-prilozenie-3-tarify-ffg-pravki-new-ru-kz-163207.pdf)

## 2. Deposits

- Methods: INVEST CARD (Freedom Bank), bank card in app, bank transfer by details, Freedom SuperApp, digital assets. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/top-up)
- **Transfer by details**: app «Меню → Пополнить → Перевод по реквизитам» (EN: Menu → Add Money → Transfer by bank details) → save/print details in the needed currency → order a transfer at any bank; **payment purpose must contain the brokerage account number**; broker charges 0%, sender bank may charge; only from the client's own account (no third parties), only from non-sanctioned banks; no minimum. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/top-up-an-account-by-bank-transfer-using-details), [minimums](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/minimum-top-up-amount2718)
- Crediting: bank transfer "on average 3 business days" (longer for FX via correspondent banks); INVEST CARD "almost instantly" (wait 10–15 min before escalating). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/terms-for-crediting-funds)
- **Card top-up**: app Menu → Add Money → "New Card"; min KZT 500 or $1; Freedom Bank cards 0%, other banks' cards **2%**; page states crediting "up to 5 business days" (attach payment order to support if late). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/by-bank-card-in-the-tradernet-mobile-app), [fees](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/terms-fees-and-comissions2716)
- **INVEST CARD** top-up is instant and free but lands on the **D-account**; move to trading account via order «Перевод между счетами». [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/top-up-with-invest-card)
- **Freedom SuperApp**: Transfers → choose brokerage account (Global accounts start with 7) → amount; credited "during the transaction day"; from 100 KZT. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/top-up-through-bank-freedom-finance-kazakhstan2717)
- Account may be funded in KZT, RUB, USD, EUR (Reg. 4.4). [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/2026/05/04/ffg-reglament-ru-en-corrected-and-revised-30042026-160747.pdf)
- Crypto funding (from Mar 2026): auto-converted to USD, 3% fee from external wallet, retail limit $1,000. [source](https://digitalbusiness.kz/2026-03-13/freedom-broker-razreshil-investoram-zavodit-dengi-cherez-kriptovalyutu/)
- Anti-fraud rule: a brokerage account can **never** be topped up by transfer to a bank card, by phone number, or to an individual's personal requisites. [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/ru-antiscam-2-134918.pdf) (Sept 2025)

## 3. Withdrawals

- Methods and app paths: «Меню → Поручения → Денежные средства → Перевод средств → На банковский счет / На банковскую карту / На криптокошелек»; INVEST CARD route = «Перевод между счетами» to the D-account (number = D + account number, e.g. D7B1234), arrives in minutes, free. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/methods-for-withdrawing-funds-from-a-brokerage-account), [PDF guide](https://fbroker.kz/file/kak-vyvesti-dengi2.pdf)
- **Timing**: to bank account — up to **5 business days** after the signed order is received (Regulation + FAQ); order counts as executed when the broker instructs the bank; onward time depends on banks/correspondents. To card — instant; if status «В обработке», extra check **up to 3 business days**. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/withdrawal-processing-time)
- Fees: to Freedom Bank 0; to other banks KZT 0, USD/EUR/AED/CNY 0.3% (min $250, max $1,000), RUB 0.3% (min $50, max $500); to other banks' cards 1% (min 300 KZT / $0.7), Freedom Bank cards 0%. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/terms---conditions--fees---commissions)
- Restrictions: only to the account holder's own bank account/card; cards must be saved in the app and previously used for top-up and **verified once** (photo of card front, CVV/expiry hidden, sent via «Меню → Поддержка → Написать в поддержку»); FX transfers need correspondent-bank details; order allowed only with no debts, no obligations, no blocked assets. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/methods-for-withdrawing-funds-from-a-brokerage-account)
- **Blocks for outdated data**: the Company may suspend order execution and withdrawal orders until the client confirms identification data, incl. a valid ID copy (Reg. 17); if **< 40 calendar days** remain on the ID, the account may be restricted. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/updating-personal-data--requestionnaire-)
- Not responding to a data request within **7 calendar days** lets the Company debit an agreed penalty up to **$100** (Reg. 17.2). [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/2026/05/04/ffg-reglament-ru-en-corrected-and-revised-30042026-160747.pdf)
- No transfers to third parties (Reg. 17.3). Euroclear "ER account" restrictions apply to Russia-linked holdings. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/er-account-causes-and-measures)

## 4. Account types

- **Trading (brokerage) account** at Freedom Finance Global PLC, AIFC; numbers start with 7. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/in-what-jurisdiction-is-a-brokerage-account-opened-2711)
- **D-счёт** = special savings sub-account opened automatically with the trading account; cash cannot be traded from it; income via overnight REPO under Annex 6 in USD/EUR/KZT, tied to SOFR/EURIBOR, accrued daily; transfers to/from trading account near-instant and free; no fee for opening/maintaining. Not covered by KDIF deposit insurance. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/savings-d-account2727), [finratings](https://finratings.kz/news/7123-kazakhstantsy-mogut-poluchat-dokhod-4-41-v-dollarakh-razbor-d-scheta-ot-freedom-broker/)

- Switching accounts: app Menu → tap «Account 7*****» label above the name; if missing, log out (Menu → Settings → Log Out), kill the app, log in. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/account-selection)
- **Demo**: the old help article on creating an extra demo account now returns 404; App Store listing doesn't mention demo. Existence of a demo mode for KZ clients today — **UNVERIFIED**.
- **ИИС** is a Russian-market product; nothing on fbroker.kz offers it — treat any "ИИС" promise as an error.
- Crypto account, professional-client status and Tabadul (ADX) access exist as separate FAQ categories. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq)

## 7. Trading

- Placing an order (app): «Котировки» → search icon → name/ticker (e.g. `AAPL.US`; ITS instruments use `.ITS`) → «Торговать» → «Купить/Продать» → price (market or limit) → quantity («max» button) → additional order settings: stop-price or margin trade → «Выставить приказ» → confirm. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/how-to-place-a-trade-order-2737)
- Order types: Market, Limit, Stop-market, Stop-limit; a market order cannot be cancelled. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/types-of-orders-in-the-trading-platform2738)
- Margin: enabled in order settings via «Использовать маржу» and stays on for later trades until unticked (Tradernet FAQ snippet; current fbroker.kz page only says "margin transaction" — exact label **UNVERIFIED**). Margin is "your right, not your obligation"; no automatic currency conversion, so withdrawing USD with only KZT creates a margin debt at 15% p.a.; no fee if repaid same day; Company may force-liquidate (Reg. 9.11; Annex 7). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/how-does-margin-loan-work-when-withdrawing-currency-)
- Access to instruments requires the «Экономический профиль» (app Menu → Requests → Personal details → Economic profile; executes in minutes). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/how-to-access-financial-instruments)
- Markets: NYSE, NASDAQ, LSE, Xetra, HKEX, KASE, AIX, ITS, Tabadul (ADX). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/what-exchanges-and-markets-are-accessed-2710)
- **Hours (Astana, GMT+5)**: KASE 11:30–17:30; AIX 11:30–17:00; NYSE/NASDAQ 18:30–01:00 (US DST) / 19:30–02:00 (winter); LSE & Xetra 12:00–20:30 / 13:00–21:30; HKEX 06:30–13:00; Freedom gives pre/post-market only one hour before/after. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/stock-market-trading-hours-), [pre/post](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/pre-and-postmarket2735) ITS: weekdays 09:00–03:45 next day, liquidity window from 16:30 (summer) / 17:30 (winter). [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/faq/question/what-are-the-trading-hours-for-its)
- **OTC / bonds via desk**: the Company provides services on regulated and OTC markets (Reg. 2.1); OTC-bought instruments can be held in a special sub-section (Reg. 4.10); fees per Annex 3 §II. Orders may be given via platform, **telephone**, authorised e-mail, in-platform tickets or model-portfolio web page (Reg. 6.3). Treasury-bond page: pick the bond, then contact your personal manager or e-mail a request stating **ISIN and quantity** (address obfuscated; footer e-mails info@fbroker.kz / 7555@fbroker.kz). A dedicated "trading desk" mailbox — **UNVERIFIED**. [source](https://fbroker.kz/en/products/kaznachejskie-obligacii)

## 8. Identification in support

- Phone/written order protocol (Reg. 6.4): client states in order (1) ФИО, (2) номер счета, (3) тикер, (4) тип заявки, (5) сторона, (6) количество, (7) цена, (8) срок; trader repeats; client says «Подтверждаю» or «Неверно». [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/2026/05/04/ffg-reglament-ru-en-corrected-and-revised-30042026-160747.pdf)
- **Голосовой пароль** is used for phone orders and as fallback identification; Latin/Cyrillic letters, digits, spaces, hyphen; "never tell it to anyone". [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/voice-password)
- Changing contact details through support requires the identification procedure / voice password; self-service change of phone (app Menu → Requests → Personal details → Change phone number, with access to old number) is instant. [source](https://fbroker.kz/faq/question/phone-number-change3818)
- Client must never share access codes "with third parties, including Company employees" (Reg. 10.5); antiscam leaflet: never give a manager your login/password or card details. An operator asking for a password/full card number/CVV is a violation. [source](https://fbroker.kz/file/ru-antiscam-2-134918.pdf)
- Calls are recorded and legally binding (Reg. 19.10–19.11); personal data protected under AIFC DPR 2017 (Reg. 19.2); no transfers to third parties (17.3). ИИН is collected at onboarding; the exact in-call script (ФИО + ИИН + address + SMS code) and the "what may not be disclosed before identification" list are internal — **UNVERIFIED**.

## 10 likely operator errors for a QA judge

1. Naming non-existent plans («Smart», «Все включено», «Премиум») or quoting old rates; the public plans are Старт/Инвестор/Стандарт/Стандарт Плюс/Международный/Инвестиционная подписка/Подписка трейдера.
2. Telling the client to see the tariff in a «Кабинет» tab of the app — it is read from the broker report («Меню → Отчеты и документы → Отчеты брокера»); in web it is «Кабинет → Отчеты брокера».
3. Promising a tariff change "immediately" — it takes effect from the next business day (Annex 3 §E).
4. Promising a bank-account withdrawal in "1–2 days" or "24 hours" — the Regulation allows up to 5 business days; card withdrawals in «В обработке» can take up to 3 business days.
5. Promising free card top-ups from any bank — 2% applies to non-Freedom Bank cards; or omitting that transfer by details requires the brokerage account number in the payment purpose.
6. Saying INVEST CARD money goes straight to the trading account — it lands on the D-account and needs «Перевод между счетами».
7. Offering to withdraw to a relative's/third-party account or to an unverified/new card — only the owner's account and a saved, previously used, verified card.
8. Giving a personal recommendation ("buy X", "D-account yields exactly 5.4%") — the Company is not obliged to advise (Reg. 2.4), returns are not guaranteed, D-rates are variable.
9. Asking for the client's password, SMS code, full card number or CVV, or suggesting a personal WhatsApp/Telegram or non-@ffin.kz e-mail — breaches Reg. 10.5 and the antiscam guide.
10. Wrong 1042-S guidance (promising a Russian/Kazakh version, promising it before 15 June, pointing to the mobile app instead of web «Кабинет → Форма 1042-S») or wrong data-change path (it is «Поручения → Персональные данные → Изменение сведений», not «Настройки»).

Also watch for: quoting support as 24/7 (official page: Mon–Fri 09:00–21:00), saying the inactivity fee still exists (abolished 9 Jan 2026), promising a complaint answer "within a week" (7 days is only the acknowledgement; decision 60–90 days), or calling the app "Tradernet.Global" without noting it is now "Freedom Broker".
